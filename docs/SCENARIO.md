# The scenario: Harbourline Facilities

Everything in this course runs against one fictional operator, so every module shows the same platform from a
different layer. Nothing here describes a real company, building, contractor or regulator; all data is synthetic.

## Harbourline Facilities

A smart-building operator with 120 commercial sites: 40 in Auckland, 25 in Wellington, 30 in Sydney and 25 in
Melbourne. Office towers, retail, logistics hubs, medical centres and mixed-use sites. Each site has a building
management system (chillers, air handling units), lifts, a fire indicator panel, access control and a smart meter.
The operations desk works from work orders, inspection schedules and standard operating procedures (SOPs).

The platform in the story is the **site operations platform**: telemetry from the buildings lands in a lakehouse and a
real-time store, the Site APIs expose it, and the **Site Operations Advisor** agent answers questions for site managers
with figures from those APIs and rules from the indexed procedures.
